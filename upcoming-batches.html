Level 1 (Essentials) introduces the foundations of solution-focused coaching, covering ICF core competencies, coaching conversations, and goal-setting frameworks. Level 2 (Advanced) deepens these skills with strategic coaching tools, integration practices, and mastery of complex coaching situations. Together, they form a complete ICF-accredited coach training pathway.

The program is designed for a wide range of participants, including:

  • Aspiring professional coaches who want to build a globally recognized ICF coaching career.
  • Business leaders and managers seeking to enhance their leadership style with powerful coaching skills.
  • HR and L&D professionals aiming to integrate coaching into talent development and organizational growth.
  • Consultants and trainers who want to expand their toolkit with solution-focused coaching methodologies.
  • Individuals on a personal growth journey who want to strengthen self-awareness, communication, and goal achievement.

Level 1 is open to all, while Level 2 is best suited for those who have completed Level 1 and are ready to deepen their skills with advanced tools and practice.

In India, both Level 1 and Level 2 are delivered fully online. Each level consists of 16 live interactive online sessions, conducted by Erickson-certified faculty. The sessions are designed to provide practical coaching practice, peer learning, and mentorship in line with Erickson’s global standards.

On completing both Level 1 and Level 2, participants meet the ICF Accredited Coach Training Program (ACTP) requirements and are eligible to apply for the ICF Associate Certified Coach (ACC) credential. This ensures global recognition of your training and coaching practice.

You can register by filling out the online application form or contacting our admissions team. Once you express interest, our team will guide you through the enrollment process, share batch schedules, and answer any questions you may have. Early registrations are encouraged as seats are limited.
