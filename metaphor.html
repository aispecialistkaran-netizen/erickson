Featured Reflections

🧭

The Compass

"For me a Coach resembles a Compass, which says 'you are free to choose the course of your life'. It assists you selflessly to sketch a detailed map with precision..."

— Ajay More, Batch-50

🦋

The Butterfly

"Just like a moth undergoes metamorphosis to transform into a beautiful butterfly, coaching empowers individuals to shed their limitations, embrace growth, and emerge as strong, confident beings."

— Ratnanjali Arora, Batch-50

💡

The Lighthouse

"A coach is a lighthouse in the vast ocean of life. A coach serves as a beacon of guidance during times of uncertainty, illuminating the path forward."

— Sushanth Ravikumar, Batch-54

🚗

The Car

"A Coach is like a car as the Coachee who is the driver decides where he or she wants to go and the Coach enables the Coachee to reach there."

— Anshul Aggarwal, Batch-50

🌳

Gardening

"Coaching is like gardening, it's something that requires patience, care, and dedication. Both involve nurturing growth, removing obstacles, and creating an environment where development/growth can happen."

— Manju Menon, Batch-56
