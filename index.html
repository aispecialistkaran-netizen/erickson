Our Legacy

Introduction

Erickson International Coaching Center, headquartered in Vancouver, Canada, was founded in 1980. Its founder, Dr. Marilyn Atkinson, holds a PhD in psychology and is a master NLP trainer. Deeply influenced by the renowned psychologist Milton Erickson, she has worked alongside numerous prominent psychology experts. Marilyn inherited and developed Erickson's academic legacy, integrating expertise from various fields to establish Erickson International Coaching Center. She pioneered a results-oriented coaching model and became a pioneer and leader in providing coaching solutions to clients.

We support organizations and individuals through results-oriented coaching and professional development programs. We have over 75,000 alumni and coaches in 179 countries and over 40 global partners. In 2025, the Erickson International Coaching Center celebrated its 45th anniversary and was awarded the 2024 ICF (International Coach Federation) Global Coach Development Influence Award!

The Erickson Edge

What makes Erickson different from
other ICF programmes

Most coach training programmes teach you what coaching is. Erickson's approach — built on solution-focused methodology — trains you in what to actually say, ask, and do inside a session.

INSIGHT 01

Solution-focused,
not problem-focused

Where many coaching frameworks ask coaches to explore what's wrong, Erickson trains coaches to orient clients toward where they want to go. This produces faster, more measurable client outcomes — and is one reason Erickson-trained coaches report higher client retention.

INSIGHT 02

A global peer network
from day one

Because cohorts are international (students from India, the Middle East, Europe and Southeast Asia often share the same cohort), you build a cross-cultural coaching perspective that's directly relevant if you plan to work with corporate clients or multinational teams.

INSIGHT 03

Methodology backed
by decades of evidence

Solution-focused coaching has one of the strongest evidence bases in the field. Erickson's curriculum is built on this foundation, not on proprietary frameworks that expire when the trend does.
