Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 1, 2025 · Last updated: May 30, 2025

At Erickson Coaching International ("Erickson", "we", "us" or "our"), your privacy is important. This page explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Types of data we collect and how we use it

We collect information required to deliver our educational services and to communicate with learners and prospects. Examples include:

  • Contact details: name, email, phone — used for course communication and support.
  • Enrollment & payment data: required to register and process tuition.
  • Course activity & usage data: to improve learning experience (anonymized when used for analytics/AI).
  • Feedback and recordings: for quality assurance and program improvement (access controlled).
We do not use personally identifiable information to train AI — only anonymized, de‑identified data is used for AI-driven improvements or analysis.

2. Our approach to anonymization & AI

AI is used responsibly and only on de‑identified data. Identifiers (name, email, payment details, IP address) are removed prior to any AI processing. Aggregated insights — not individual profiles — guide product and content improvements.

3. How we use your information

  • Deliver coaching and training services and related communications.
  • Manage enrollment, billing and accreditation reporting.
  • Improve our programs and website using anonymized analytics.
  • Comply with legal or regulatory obligations.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We may share data with trusted service providers (payment processors, hosting, CRM/LMS providers) under contract and only as necessary. We may disclose information when required by law.

5. Data security

We implement administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal data. While no system is completely secure, we take prompt action when risks are identified — and we encourage you to contact us if you suspect a breach.

6. International transfers

Your information may be processed or stored in jurisdictions with different data protection laws. Where transfers occur we require service providers to meet applicable data protection standards.

7. Your rights & choices

You may have the right to access, correct, export or request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal and contractual retention requirements. To exercise these rights, contact our Privacy Officer (details below).

8. Cookies & tracking technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance site functionality, measure site usage and personalise content. You can control cookies through your browser settings or our cookie preferences panel (where available).

9. Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfil operational, legal, or accreditation requirements (typically up to seven years unless otherwise required by law).

10. Third‑party links

Our site may link to third‑party services; we are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their policies before providing information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in law or our practices. Material changes will be posted here and, where appropriate, notified directly.

12. Children & youth

Our services are not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children without parental consent.

13. Global SMS consent

By providing your phone number you consent to receive SMS/MMS communications related to your programs and services. You can opt out at any time (reply STOP or contact us).

14. Contact

If you have questions or wish to exercise your privacy rights, contact our Privacy Officer at support@erickson.co.in or support@erickson.co.in.

This privacy notice is based on the public Erickson Coaching International policy (erickson.co.in) and adapted for Erickson Coaching India.
