Our Founder

Erickson Coaching International was founded in 1980 in Vancouver by psychologist and coach Dr. Marilyn Atkinson. For more than four decades, the organisation has pioneered solution‑focused coach training that emphasizes the resourcefulness of every individual. Today Erickson’s certified programs are delivered on every continent and recognised by the International Coaching Federation (ICF).

With over 75,000 alumni across 130+ countries and more than 40 strategic partners worldwide, Erickson continues to expand its reach. Erickson India brings this legacy to India, offering professional coach training, corporate programs and personal development courses that empower leaders, teams and organisations.

Gaurav Arora

We Are Bringing The Global Coaching Community To You.

Gaurav Arora is a Master Certified Coach (MCC) with the International Coaching Federation (ICF) and serves as an international partner and faculty member with Erickson Coaching International. His philosophy — "Who you are is how you Coach" — reinforces our core belief that authentic, solution‑focused coaching creates profound transformation.

Over the years, Gaurav has guided thousands on their coaching journeys, helping them connect authentically and make a meaningful difference. As a passionate advocate for India's coaching community, his promise is simple: 1% MORE commitment to your coaching journey than your commitment to yourself.

Four Pillars of Erickson’s Coaching Solution

Future & Solutions Focused

Coaching conversations centre on what clients want to create. We focus on goals and desired outcomes instead of analysing past problems.

Holistic & Systemic

People live and work within systems. Our approach considers individual, team and organisational contexts to ensure lasting change.

Resource Activation

We trust clients’ creativity and resourcefulness. Coaching surfaces talents and strengths, building confidence and self‑leadership.

Action & Accountability

Insight alone is not enough. Erickson coaches support clients to design tangible actions and maintain momentum toward their goals.
